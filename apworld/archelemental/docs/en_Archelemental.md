# Archelemental

## What is this game?

Archelemental is Elemental, a periodic table roll game, made for Archipelago. Every roll pulls elements from the
periodic table, with a chance to chain into compounds. The rarer an element, the less often it turns up.

## What are the checks?

- Finding each of the 118 elements for the first time. A compound counts for every element in it, so rolling
  H2O checks both hydrogen and oxygen.
- Finding each of the 16 joke elements, if they're on.
- Getting one of each catalyst from your own shop or a drop, up to the tier set in your options.
- Each of the 100 compound achievements, if they're on (off by default: it's a lot of checks).

## What gets sent to other players' worlds, and what do I get?

Your checks can hold anything from the multiworld. What you receive:

- **Progressive Luck**, which tilts your rolls toward rarer elements. The logic waits for enough of it before
  expecting you to find the rare ones.
- **Joke Table**, which lets joke elements turn up in your rolls.
- **Autoroll**, **Chain Boost**, **Extra Roll** and **Shop Slot**, which speed things up.
- Catalysts and Quark Bundles as filler.

## What's the goal?

Find the number of elements (and joke elements) set in your options.
