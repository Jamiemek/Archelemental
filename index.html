How a roll works

Each element has its own odds. Rarer elements come up less often, and luck bonuses nudge the weighting toward them.

After every element there is a 1 in 8 chance of adding another, up to 30. Four rare elements and four deep bond lines each improve that, up to 1 in 5.

A chain is written as one formula in Hill order: carbon first, then hydrogen, then the rest alphabetically.

Gameplay

Active catalysts

Cancelling a catalyst stops it immediately and it is gone for good. There is no refund and this cannot be undone. You can also cancel them from the inventory.

Joke periodic table

Joke elements roll alongside the real ones. With mixing on they can chain with real elements into joke compounds. With it off, a chain is all joke or all real: a joke element that turns up in a chain takes the roll on its own. Your chances of finding them are the same either way.

Save and storage

Storage

If your save gets full, clear old finds you no longer need. Every find scoring below the minimum you choose is removed, except single elements, achievement formulas, your top 10s, and anything your bonds still need. Your roll total, elements found and achievements don't change.

Start over

Delete your collection, found elements and achievements. This cannot be undone.
